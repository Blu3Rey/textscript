---
title: Three Sum
inputs: nums
split: train
tags: array, sorting, two-pointers
---
Given a list of integers `nums`, return every triple of values taken from
three different positions whose sum is zero, as a list of three-element
lists. No triple of values may appear twice in the answer; the order of the
triples doesn't matter.

Example: for `nums = [-2, 0, 1, 1, 2]` the answer is
`[[-2, 0, 2], [-2, 1, 1]]`.
