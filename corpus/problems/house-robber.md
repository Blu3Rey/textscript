title: House Robber
inputs: nums
split: test
tags: array, dynamic-programming

A street has a row of houses, and `nums[i]` is the amount of money in house
`i`. Money can be taken from any set of houses as long as no two of them are
next to each other. Return the largest total that can be taken.

Example: for `nums = [2, 7, 9, 3, 1]` the answer is `12`.
