---
title: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock
inputs: prices
split: train
tags: array
---
`prices` is a list of a stock's price on consecutive days. You may buy one
share on one day and sell it on a strictly later day. Return the largest
profit that a single buy and sell can make, or `0` if no trade makes money.

Example: for `prices = [7, 1, 5, 3, 6, 4]` the answer is `5`.
