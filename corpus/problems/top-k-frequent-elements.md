---
title: Top K Frequent Elements
inputs: nums, k
split: train
tags: array, hash-map, counting
---
Given a list of integers `nums` and an integer `k`, return the `k` distinct
values that occur most often in `nums`, in any order. You may assume the
answer is unique: there is never a tie at the boundary of the top `k`.

Example: for `nums = [5, 7, 5, 9, 7, 5]` and `k = 2`, the answer is
`[5, 7]`.
