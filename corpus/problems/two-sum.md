---
title: Two Sum
inputs: nums, target
tags: array, hash-map
---
Given a list of integers `nums` and an integer `target`, return the indices
of two different elements whose sum is `target`, as a list of two indices.
You may assume exactly one such pair exists.

Example: for `nums = [2, 7, 11, 15]` and `target = 9`, the answer is
`[0, 1]`.
