---
title: Container With Most Water
inputs: height
split: train
tags: array, two-pointers
---
`height` is a list of non-negative integers; `height[i]` is the height of a
vertical line standing at position `i`. Any two lines, together with the
ground, form a container. The water it holds is the distance between the
two positions times the height of the lower line. Return the largest amount
of water any pair of lines can hold.

Example: for `height = [2, 3, 4, 5, 18, 17, 6]` the answer is `17`.
