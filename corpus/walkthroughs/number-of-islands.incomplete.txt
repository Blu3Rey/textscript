Keep a set called visited for the cells we've already explored.
Write a function explore that takes row and col.
If the cell is off the grid, or grid[row][col] is zero, or the pair row, col is in visited, just return.
Otherwise put the pair in visited and call explore on row plus one, row minus one, col plus one and col minus one.
Then loop r over the indices of grid and c over the indices of grid[r], and if grid[r][c] is one and the pair r, c isn't in visited, add one to islands and explore from r, c.
