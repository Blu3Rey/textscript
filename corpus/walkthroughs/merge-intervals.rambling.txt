Okay, so, um, the thing is, overlapping intervals are only easy to spot when they're in order, right?
So first I sort the intervals by their start time, um, smallest first.
Then I have a result list, I'll call it out, and it starts empty.
I put the first interval into out, um, just to get going.
Then I loop through the rest of the intervals, from the second one on, calling each one cur, and, uh, I call the last one in out last.
If cur's start is at most last's end, they overlap, so, um, last's end becomes the max of the two ends.
Otherwise, um, no overlap, so I just add cur to the end of out.
And then I return out, and, uh, the sort makes it n log n overall.
