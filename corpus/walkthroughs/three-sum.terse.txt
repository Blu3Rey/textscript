problem: three-sum
style: terse
---
Sort nums. Result list res.
Loop i over the indices; skip when i > 0 and nums[i] equals nums[i - 1].
lo is i + 1, hi is the last index.
While lo < hi, total is nums[i] + nums[lo] + nums[hi].
Total under zero, lo += 1; over zero, hi -= 1.
Else append [nums[i], nums[lo], nums[hi]] to res, lo += 1, hi -= 1, then while lo < hi and nums[lo] == nums[lo - 1], lo += 1.
Return res.
