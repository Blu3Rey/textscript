Okay so, um, we buy on one day and sell on some later day, and the brute force is like checking every pair of days, which is n squared, so let's not.
Instead I'll, uh, keep track of the cheapest price so far, call it lowest, and it starts as the very first price.
And, um, best is the best profit so far, and that starts at zero.
So then I walk through the prices, one at a time.
For each price, um, I work out profit as the price minus lowest, and if profit is bigger than best, best becomes profit.
And also, uh, if the price is lower than lowest, then lowest becomes the price.
And at the end, um, return best, and that's it, one pass, so O of n.
