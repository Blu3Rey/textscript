First I'd sort the numbers.
Then go over each index i of nums.
For each one, left starts at i plus one and right starts at the end of the array.
While left is below right, add up nums[i], nums[left] and nums[right] into total.
If total is zero, append those three numbers as a list to result, and move left up one and right down one.
Otherwise, if it's below zero, move left up one, and if not, move right down one.
Oh, and duplicates are a concern, the same triple can't show up twice.
