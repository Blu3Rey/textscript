I'll go level by level, with a deque called queue that starts with root in it.
I also keep a counter called depth.
While the queue is not empty, add one to depth, then repeat len(queue) times to handle one level.
Each time, popleft from queue into node, and if node.left is not None append it, and same for node.right.
Once the queue runs out, return depth.
