---
problem: house-robber
style: incomplete
---
Make a list dp the same length as nums, filled with zeros.
If there's only one house, just return the money in it.
dp[0] is just nums[0], and dp[1] is the bigger of nums[0] and nums[1].
Then for every index i of nums from 2 onward, dp[i] is the max of dp[i - 1] and dp[i - 2] plus nums[i].
That's O(n) time and O(n) space.
