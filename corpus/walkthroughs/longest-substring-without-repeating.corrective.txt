I'll keep a list called window with the characters in the current window.
Left starts at zero, and best starts at one.
Actually, make window a set instead, so checking membership is fast.
And best should start at zero, not one.
Then for each index right in range of len of s, while s[right] is in window, remove s[left] from window and bump left by one.
After that, add s[right] to window and set best to the max of best and right minus left plus one.
Return best at the end.
