Make a list called answer, the same length as nums, filled with ones.
Keep a running product called left.
Go left to right over the indices i of nums: set answer at i to left, then multiply left by nums at i.
Then a second running product called right, starting at one.
Go from the last index down to zero, multiply answer at i by right, and then multiply right by nums at i.
Return answer.
