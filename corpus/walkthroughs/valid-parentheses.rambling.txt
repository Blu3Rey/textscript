Okay, so, um, brackets have to close in the reverse order they opened, which, uh, kind of screams stack to me.
So I'll have a stack, which is just an empty list, and, um, a dictionary called pairs where close paren maps to open paren, close square to open square, and close curly to open curly.
Then, uh, I go through the string one character at a time, and I'll call it c.
If c is in pairs, so it's a closing one, um, then if the stack is empty or the top of the stack isn't pairs of c, return false.
Otherwise, um, it matched, so I pop the top off the stack.
And for anything else, so the openers, I just push it onto the stack.
And at the very end, um, it's only valid if nothing's left, so I return whether the stack is empty.
That's O of n time, and, uh, O of n space for the stack.
