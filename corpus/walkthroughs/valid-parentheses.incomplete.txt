Stack is an empty list, and pairs is a dictionary mapping close paren to open paren, close square to open square, and close curly to open curly.
Loop over each character ch in s.
If ch is in pairs, it's a closer, so pop the stack, and if what we popped isn't pairs of ch, return false.
Otherwise push ch onto the stack.
At the end, return True if the stack is empty.
