lo is zero and hi is the last index.
While lo is less than or equal to hi, take mid as lo plus hi floor-divided by two.
Actually make the loop condition strictly less than, otherwise it never stops.
If nums[mid] is greater than nums[hi], the minimum is somewhere to the right of mid, so lo becomes mid plus one.
Otherwise hi becomes mid minus one.
No, hi should become just mid, because mid itself could be the minimum.
