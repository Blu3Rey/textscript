I'll use two pointers, left at the start of the string and right at its last character.
While left is less than right, keep going.
If the character at left isn't alphanumeric, move left forward one and skip to the next round.
Same check for right, except right moves back one.
Then if the lowercase versions of the two characters are different, return false.
Otherwise move left up one and right down one.
