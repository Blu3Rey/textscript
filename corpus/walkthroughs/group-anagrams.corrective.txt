I'll keep a dictionary called groups that maps a key to the words sharing that key.
For every word in strs, I build a list called count with twenty-six zeros, one slot per letter.
Then for each character c in the word, add one to count at ord of c minus ord of "a".
The key is just count itself.
Oh wait, a list can't be a dictionary key, so make the key the tuple of count instead.
If the key isn't in groups yet, map it to an empty list, and then append the word to groups at the key.
At the end, return the values of groups, converted to a list.
